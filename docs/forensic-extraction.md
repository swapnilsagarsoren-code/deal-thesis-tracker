# Forensic Extraction — line items per claim type

**Owner: Swapnil.** This list must come from his audit experience. The items below are the starting draft from planning; he should edit, add, and remove before the build.

## Cost-synergy claims

- Cost of goods sold (COGS) and gross margin trend
- SG&A / other expenses as % of revenue
- Employee benefit expense and headcount (where disclosed)
- Procurement / raw material cost per unit (where disclosed)
- Integration or one-time restructuring costs (so savings are not overstated)

## Revenue-synergy claims

- Segment revenue for the acquired business
- Cross-sell or bundled product disclosures
- Customer concentration changes
- Market share claims vs third-party data

## Universal checks (every deal)

- **Goodwill impairment** disclosures — an audit-native red flag that the price paid was not justified by results
- Related-party transactions linked to the deal *(optional)*
- Post-deal leverage and debt covenants *(optional)*
