Checked by text search of the source PDFs (deal deck, 1 Mar 2023 call, Q4FY24/Q1FY25/Q2FY25/Q4FY25 calls, full annual reports FY23–FY26). "PDF p." = page index in the file; printed page given where it differs.

**Page offsets (printed vs PDF):** FY23 AR printed = PDF − 2. FY24 AR printed = PDF. FY25 AR printed = PDF (checked at p.238). FY26 AR printed = PDF + 1. Deck and transcripts printed = PDF.

## Original estimate (what Axis promised)
- "We had estimated Rs 2,000 crores pre-tax, Rs 1,500 crores post-tax. We stay with that estimate. And that expense will be incurred over 18 months period." — 1 Mar 2023 call, p.8. Call/management commentary.
- Repeated: "current best estimate of the transitional costs and integration expenses is ~₹2,000 crores" — FY23 AR printed p.107 (PDF p.109). MD&A.
- Start date: "The current best estimate of the transitional period is 18 months commencing from 1 March, 2023" — FY23 AR printed p.106 (PDF p.108). MD&A. Window = 1 Mar 2023 to 31 Aug 2024. (Settles the "1 April 2023, for discussion purposes" wording on call p.10.)
- **Different basis disclosed at deal time:** deck p.15 shows ₹1,500 cr "post tax impact over 2 financial years", not 18 months.
- **Scope:** deck p.15 note 3: "Integration expenses includes transition services agreement expenses to be paid to Citigroup or its Affiliates and Axis Bank's own cost." Call p.12: TSA ≈ "70% to 75%" of the estimate, charged on a straight-line basis.

## The estimate was never referred to again after FY23 (verified 11 Oct)
- Text search of all 11 reviewed documents for the ₹2,000 cr estimate: it appears only in the 1 Mar 2023 call (p.8) and the FY23 AR (printed p.107 / PDF p.109).
- It does not appear in the FY24, FY25 or FY26 annual reports, or in the Q4FY24, Q1FY25, Q2FY25 or Q4FY25 calls. No document compares actual integration cost with the estimate.
- Label: "Estimate not referred to after FY23; no comparison disclosed." Not a failure finding — costs may have come in under the estimate; the filings don't say.
- Note: in the blind AI test, Haiku quoted the restated estimate rather than the ₹217 cr actual from the same FY23 paragraph. That is model selection behaviour, not evidence about Axis — both figures sit side by side in the source.
- Re-run 12 Oct on the page files of all 11 documents: same result. Later ₹2,000 cr / ₹1,500 cr hits (FY26 AR PDF p.107, 147, 209, 211) are a fund launch and bonds, not this estimate. The completion announcement and the Q4FY25 call do not mention integration costs.
- This finding rests on the text search, not on the AI disclosure timeline. In live runs the finder did not return the FY23 restatement, so the timeline is hidden on public cards (decision log row 37).

## Management declined to give numbers when asked
- Q1FY25 call (24 Jul 2024), p.18: analyst Anand Dama asked for retention ratios and integration costs.
- Answer (Arjun Chowdhry): "consistent with the earlier quarters too, we don't share specific numbers" — a standing policy, not a one-off. He then said every metric set out at acquisition was "met or exceeded".
- Context for fairness: on the 1 Mar 2023 call p.7, the CEO said "Right now we have not decided what we'll share and how much we'll share." Axis never committed to publish integration numbers.

Neither disclosed a Citi/Citibank portfolio result or an acquisition-specific update on cards, Burgundy, customer migration, retention, transition services, integration costs or synergies. The presentation's Burgundy AUM (PDF p.32 / slide 22) and credit-card disclosures (PDF p.35 / slide 25) are bank-wide; they do not identify the acquired Citi book.
- The [Axis call-transcript archive](https://www.axis.bank.in/shareholders-corner/other-information/call-transcript) had Q2 opening remarks but no full Q&A transcript when checked on 20 Oct; the [Axis audio-call archive](https://www.axis.bank.in/shareholders-corner/other-information/audio-call-transcript) links the recordings. I reviewed the full [Q2 earnings-call recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JkVHKDMcutQ) (61:28) and [media-call recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KRvyzfIvVU) (36:42), using English auto-captions as a search aid for Citi/Citibank, integration, cards, Burgundy/wealth, migration, costs, retention, attrition and synergies. - **Do not add** the Q2 Burgundy or card figures to the Citi series, or describe them as Citi-driven. The results page and call recordings above are Axis Bank's primary sources.

### What the earlier management record supports
- **Execution:** Axis management reported final customer migration on 14 Jul 2024, two months ahead of schedule ([Q1 FY25 earnings-call transcript](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/call-transcript/q1-fy25-media-conference-call-transcript.pdf), p.4). This is a management-reported integration milestone, not a financial-return measure.
- **Cost:** Axis estimated about ₹2,000 cr pre-tax over 18 months ([FY23 annual report](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/annual-reports/for-axis-bank/annual-report-for-the-year-2022-2023.pdf), PDF p.109). It later disclosed ₹1,323 cr for months 1–13 through March 2024 ([FY24 integrated annual report](https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/reports-and-disclosures/annual-disclosures/integrated-annual-report-fy-2023-24.pdf?sfvrsn=c0a712a2_1), p.212). FY25 reported costs were partially offset by a reduction in integration costs ([FY25 annual report](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/annual-reports/for-axis-bank/annual-report-for-the-year-2024-2025.pdf), p.238); FY26 retained the same non-quantified wording ([FY26 annual report](https://www.axis.bank.in/annual-reports/2025-2026/index.html), PDF p.245). The remaining months 14–18 have no disclosed amount or final total/variance; see the cost table above. The 18-month window ended 31 Aug 2024.
- **Synergies:** The original public target was savings of 30–40% of Citi's CY2020 operating costs within two years ([acquisition presentation](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/investor-presentations/presentation-axis-bank-acquisition-of-citibank-consumer-businesses-in-india.pdf), p.16). In Q4 FY25, management said synergies were a bit ahead of an internal six-quarter Board plan, then said it no longer separately monitored the Citi and Axis books ([Q4 FY25 earnings-call transcript](https://www.axisbank.com/docs/default-source/call-transcript/q4fy25-earnings-call-transcript.pdf), p.13). No public bridge reconciles that internal-plan statement to the original target.

### Verdict
Operational integration has a positive management-reported marker: customer migration finished ahead of schedule. But Q2 FY27 adds no Citi-specific financial metric, the disclosed integration-cost series is incomplete, and management's internal synergy comparison is not reconciled to the public target. **On public evidence, whether the ₹11,603 cr acquisition is paying off remains unverified; the absence of a Citi-specific Q2 disclosure is not evidence of failure.**
